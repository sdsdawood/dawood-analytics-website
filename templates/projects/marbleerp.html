Project Overview

MarbleERP is a complete business management system designed for marble, granite and trading businesses. The system combines accounting, inventory, sales, purchasing and reporting into a single integrated platform. It was developed to replace manual processes and provide business owners with accurate information for better decisions.

Business Problems

  • Manual bookkeeping and accounting processes
  • Difficulty tracking inventory movement
  • Separated sales, purchase and financial records
  • Delayed business reporting and analysis
  • Limited visibility into business performance

Solution

MarbleERP provides a complete integrated ERP environment where business operations are connected through a single system. The solution combines accounting, inventory management, sales, purchasing and reporting modules to improve accuracy, reduce manual work and provide better business visibility.

Core Features

  • Accounting Management: Double-entry accounting, ledger management, financial statements and reporting.
  • Inventory Management: Stock tracking, item records, inventory movement and availability monitoring.
  • Sales Management: Sales transactions, customer records, invoice management and sales reporting.
  • Purchase Management: Supplier management, purchase records and procurement tracking.
  • Business Reports: Financial reports, summaries and analytical insights for decision making.
