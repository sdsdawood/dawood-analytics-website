Project Overview

Retail Analytics is a business intelligence solution designed to help retail businesses understand their sales performance through data analysis, automated reporting and interactive visualizations. The platform processes raw sales data and converts it into actionable insights including product performance, sales trends, customer behavior and key business metrics.

Business Problems

  • Manual preparation of sales reports using spreadsheets
  • Difficulty identifying top-performing products and categories
  • Limited visibility into sales trends and business KPIs
  • Time-consuming data cleaning and reporting processes
  • Lack of automated analytical dashboards for decision making

Analytics Solution

Retail Analytics provides an end-to-end data analysis workflow that transforms raw business data into meaningful insights. The solution includes data cleaning, transformation, statistical analysis, visualization and automated reporting to support faster and more accurate business decisions.

Key Features

  • Sales Performance Analysis: Analysis of revenue, sales trends and business performance.
  • Product Analysis: Identification of top-selling products, categories and product contribution.
  • KPI Dashboards: Interactive dashboards for monitoring important business indicators.
  • Automated Reporting: Generation of structured business reports with reduced manual effort.
  • Data Processing Pipeline: Cleaning, transformation and preparation of raw datasets for analysis.
